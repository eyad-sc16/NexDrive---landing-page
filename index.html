Most operators are live within 2-4 weeks. Our onboarding team handles hardware commissioning, network configuration, and driver app setup. For enterprise fleet deployments, we assign a dedicated solutions engineer.

Yes. All NexDrive infrastructure runs on AWS Middle East (Bahrain) region. We comply with UAE and Saudi data residency regulations. No user or operational data leaves the region.
